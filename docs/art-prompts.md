# Art sheets — Sundown Kennels Simulator

Generated from the game's own data tables by `scripts/make-art-prompts.mjs`.

**Each block below is one complete prompt that produces six images.** Paste a
block, get a sheet back, send me the sheet and I will cut it into the six files
named above it and wire them in. Nothing needs pasting in front of anything.

Work top to bottom. The sheets are ordered so that stopping at any point still
leaves the game better than it was — the dog breeds first, because that is what
a player looks at most.

**On style:** flat vector rather than photoreal. Not taste — photoreal AI animals
drift in lighting, camera and detail between generations, and two hundred of them
will not look like one game. Flat art with a fixed brief holds together, matches
the item icons already in the game, and reads at the size these are actually shown.

---
